#### McClintock Letters: One Year Later

Visit the [Innovations in Science website](https://innovationsinscience.com/) to learn more, support, or get involved with this new project in partnership with local newspapers developing a model for community-oriented science communication to carry on the mission of the [McClintock Letters](https://snapcoalition.org/initiatives/mcclintock-letters/).

To further continue this work, we will be organizing training sessions and encouraging interested citizens to write locally-focused opinion pieces about the proposed changes to the OMB's Proposed Federal Financial Assistance Rule.

#### Congressional District Visits: One Year Later

Building on the success of the [SNAP Congressional District Visits initiative](https://snapcoalition.org/initiatives/district-visits-2025/) that took place during the 2025 August recess, there will be a campaign of local district visits with Representatives organized during the 2026 August recess. Be on the lookout for more information coming soon!

#### Stance on Science

Stance on Science participants are asking science policy-related questions of candidates running in local, state, and national races during the 2026 elections. State teams have started to develop their questionnaires, plan candidate outreach, and set up town halls in their communities. **This is a great time to get involved and make sure science policy issues important to your community are included as teams begin to contact campaigns, especially with primaries approaching and underway in many states!**

Check out responses we've already received from candidates across several states at the [Stance on Science website](https://snapcoalition.org/initiatives/stance-on-science).
