#### 2026 SNAP Congressional District Visits

So far, SNAP has had ~35 successful [congressional district visits](https://snapcoalition.org/initiatives/district-visits-2026/)! This year, visits are particularly **focused on the proposed [OMB](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/) (Office of Management and Budget) federal financial assistance rule** ([Docket OMB-2026-0034](https://www.regulations.gov/docket/OMB-2026-0034)) and its potential impacts on scientific researchers and the public. In partnership with the Stance on Science Initiative, we are doing an op-ed push, kicked off by hosting at [Q&A info session](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/wnNeJ_WnSGumR80brV76EQ) with a former NYT op-ed editor Clay Risen on Sept. 24 @ 12 p.m. ET. We will also host three op-ed writing sessions in the upcoming weeks, [sign-up here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfeRmD1YYFLeErNy80NA7SQ-Jc3cPGUOO1LSG5NINI560WXLA/viewform?usp=header) to receive more info about those.

#### Science Policy in a SNAP - SNAP Blog

You can access our blog, Science Policy in a SNAP, [on our website](https://snapcoalition.org/blog/) or give us a follow [on Medium](https://medium.com/science-policy-in-a-snap), where you'll hear from a variety of SNAP member voices on diverse science and science-policy topics.

Emma Scales kicked off the month with a post on **["The need for farmer-forward sustainable livestock policy, not cheap foreign beef"](https://medium.com/science-policy-in-a-snap/the-need-for-farmer-forward-sustainable-livestock-policy-not-cheap-foreign-beef-2cbc98c94d9d)**, discussing agricultural policies that can help incentivize sustainable practices. Sneha Rao reflected on what inspires her to pursue basic science and how we might better support this type of research moving forward in her post, **["It's a Wonderful Life"](https://medium.com/science-policy-in-a-snap/its-a-wonderful-life-afe73bbf079e)**. Last week, Jessa Burling explored the shifting research and policy landscape of psychedelics in the US in her post, **["A Trip Down Memory Lane: Psychedelics & Their Complicated Status Throughout United States Health Policy"](https://medium.com/science-policy-in-a-snap/a-trip-down-memory-lane-psychedelics-their-complicated-status-throughout-united-states-health-b49e9e40242f)**.

Be sure to [follow us on Medium](https://medium.com/science-policy-in-a-snap) to read the upcoming pieces!

#### Stance on Science

Stance on Science participants are asking science policy-related questions of candidates running in local, state, and national races during the 2026 elections. State teams are developing questionnaires, doing candidate outreach, and setting up town halls in their communities. **This is a great time to get involved and make sure science policy issues important to your community are included.**

We had received over 2,000 responses from more than 400 candidates across 30 states, view responses on the [Stance on Science website](https://snapcoalition.org/initiatives/stance-on-science). We are [receiving media attention](https://www.wmdt.com/2026/09/grassroots-initiative-bringing-candidates-stances-on-science-issues/) and are continuing to share the resource we are creating.

If you live in Georgia, there are two opportunities to hear candidates answer Stance questions live at town halls.

- Athens, GA Forum on Energy and the Environment at the ACC Library Auditorium on 2025 Baxter Street on October 6th from 6 - 7 pm RSVP at [https://bit.ly/GAFallForum](https://bit.ly/GAFallForum)
- Valdosta Down Ballot Resource Fair and Forum at Big Nick's Juke Joint 1201 Baytree Road on October 17th at 6pm
